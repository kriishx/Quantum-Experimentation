# Quantum Experimentation

A hands-on journey into **Quantum Computing** through learning, experimentation, and implementation.

This repository contains my experiments, implementations, and learning work as I explore the foundations of quantum computing and gradually move toward more advanced concepts using tools such as **Qiskit**.

The goal of this repository is not just to study quantum computing theoretically, but to understand the underlying concepts by **building, simulating, testing, and experimenting with them in code**.

---

## 🧠 What is this Repository?

This repository serves as my personal **Quantum Computing experimentation lab**.

It contains implementations covering different aspects of quantum computing, starting from fundamental concepts and gradually moving toward more practical and complex quantum systems.

The work is organized into:

* Quantum computing fundamentals
* Qiskit implementations
* Quantum circuits and gates
* Quantum algorithms and experiments
* Quantum computer architecture concepts
* Custom quantum computer experimentation

As I continue learning, this repository will evolve with new implementations and experiments.

## 🖥️ Quantum Computer Builder

The **Quantum Computer Builder** directory focuses on understanding quantum computing from a more fundamental and architectural perspective.

Instead of treating a quantum computer as a black box, the goal is to experiment with the concepts and components that make quantum computation possible.

This section will gradually explore:

* Quantum computing architecture
* Qubit representation
* Quantum gates
* Quantum circuits
* Measurement systems
* Quantum control concepts
* Quantum hardware concepts
* Simulation and experimentation

The long-term objective is to develop a stronger understanding of the relationship between **quantum algorithms, quantum circuits, and the underlying quantum computing architecture**.

## 🔬 Experimentation Philosophy

The approach followed in this repository is:

```text
Learn the concept
      ↓
Understand the mathematics
      ↓
Build the quantum circuit
      ↓
Simulate / Execute
      ↓
Observe the results
      ↓
Analyze the behaviour
      ↓
Experiment further
```

The objective is to bridge the gap between **quantum computing theory and practical implementation**.
